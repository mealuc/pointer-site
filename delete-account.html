Delete your account

This deletes your Pointer account and everything in it, permanently. You do not need to have the app installed — verify who you are below and the deletion happens immediately.

What gets deleted, right away and for good:

  • Your profile — name, date of birth, gender
  • Every profile and outfit photo you uploaded
  • Your location posts and their tags
  • Your matches, match requests and conversations
  • Your login credentials

There is no undo, and no grace period. The same account cannot be restored later.
