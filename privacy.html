Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pointer collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Account information: email address and password.
  • Profile information: name, surname, gender, date of birth.
  • Photos: profile and outfit photos you choose to upload, which may include images of your face.
  • Location: your location when you share a post (used to verify the accuracy of the shared location) and when you discover posts nearby.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send to other users.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To enable location-based discovery and matching.
  • To display your profile and posts to other users.
  • To facilitate messaging between matched users.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, storage).
  • Google Sign-In (optional login).
  • Apple Sign-In (optional login).
  • SendGrid (sending support emails when you contact us).

These services process data on our behalf according to their own privacy policies.

4. DATA SHARING

We do not sell your personal data. Your profile information and posts are visible to other users of the app as part of its core functionality.

5. LOCAL DEVICE STORAGE

Some information is stored locally on your device to make the app work smoothly:

  • Your last known location coordinates (to show nearby posts without re-requesting GPS each time).
  • Saved/bookmarked posts.
  • App preferences such as whether you have seen the onboarding and location prompts.

This local data stays on your device and is removed when you delete your account or uninstall the app.

6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

You can delete your account at any time from the profile menu. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos, and messages are permanently removed, and local data on your device is cleared.

7. SECURITY

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. CHILDREN

This app is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

9. CHANGES

We may update this policy from time to time. We will reflect the latest update date at the top of this document.

10. CONTACT

For privacy questions, please use the Help & Support option in the app.
